Chemical elements in Cantonese

This is the name of every Chemical Elements in Cantonese Quóc Ngữ

Doctorine: Any element which have its Chinese Translation after 1937 will not longer use Chinese word to represent it Cantonese Name. Such as Technetium and Americium.
Those Elements which discovered at accient time will remain the Cantonese Words. Such as Thùng for Copper and Thiết for Iron.
Those Elements which is discovered later and translated by Wu Chemist will be remained, if the pronunciation in Cantonese is reasonable. Such as Dưỡng for Oxygen and Hinh for Hydrogen.
If a Chinese name of Element have already translated into Cantonese and wide spread, this sino-cantonese word will be accepted. Such as Khói for Calcium and Káp for Potassium.
If the pronunciation of Chinese Character in Cantonese is too far away from origin, this Chinese Character will be abolished, such as Antimony and Beryllium.
If the pronunciation of Chinese Character in Cantonese is causing contradiction, this Chinese Character will be abolished, such as Cadmium and Chromium.
If an Element has already developed a native Cantonese Name, this name will be accepted, such as Thây for Aluminium and Sủi-Ngần for Mercury.
If the Chinese Translation for an element is accepted, the spelling in Cantonese will no longer follow the rule of sino-cantonese word. Such as Mỡi for Magnesium and Đỉn for Iodine.
The Cantonese Translation for those elements will look at its name in Latin, English, Japanese, and Vietnamese.
