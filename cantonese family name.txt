Family Surnames in Cantonese
This is the list of Family Surname in Cantonese, Transcription from Chinese Characters to Cantonese National Language Script
This list included the most famous 411 family name from Hundred Family Surnames(Bách Ka Ŧinh), and those very common family surnames in the Canton and Vietnam.
In the Family Surnames of Cantonese, the antique spelling will be adopted such as "Tr" and "Vuòng". In order to show the Etymology and differentiate from Homophones.
The Transcription of Surnames is using the "universal version" of Cantonese National Language Script as possible, while the dialect, language and habit can also affect the spelling.
The un-cantonese speaker with a name in Chinese Characters may have another name of transliteration according to the pronunciation of his native language, instead of have a Cantonese Transcription, depending on his preference.
This list of Family Surname will help the government of Cantonese speaking nation to manage the population.
One Surname in Chinese Character may have several transcriptions in Cantonese National Language Script. The spelling should also follow the preference of every person.
